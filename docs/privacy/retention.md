# Retention (D17.3)

How long personal data is kept and what removes it. The data itself and the deletion outcome per column are in [`data-inventory.md`](data-inventory.md). Guarded by `RetentionTableTest` (every history action listed with its policy, every policy explained, every `CleanupTask` listed, every remover named here exists) and `DataInventoryTest` (every documented table with an expiry column is listed below).

Review against plan §10.1 ("pickup sign-ups kept 2 years, personal data changes as long as the account exists, anonymised on deletion") and §17 (data minimisation, deletion with anonymisation and archive) on 2026-10-29, decision 87:

- Pickup events of the pickup module (`AUTO_CONFIRMED`, `AUTO_DECLINED`, `EXTRA_SLOT_ADDED`) were FOREVER; they are sign-up events and are now YEARS_2 like `SIGN_UP_SLOT`.
- Pure preferences and delivery logs were FOREVER: FSP `FOLLOWED`/`UNFOLLOWED`, group `MAIL_SENT`, poll `NOTIFIED` and OAuth `TOKEN_ISSUED` (a login-like event) are now YEARS_2.
- `ACCOUNT_LIFETIME` was never applied: account deletion now clears values, reason and metadata of these entries about the user (`HistoryRetentionService.anonymiseAccountLifetime`). `ACCOUNT_DELETED` itself is YEARS_2, like the account archive.
- Expiring personal data without a cleanup: ended sessions, expired email changes, ended IP blocks, sign-up attempts (`AuthRecordsCleanup`) and the deleted-account archive (`UserArchiveCleanup`) are now removed.
- Governance traces (region/group/store membership changes, reports, forum moderation, content, verification) stay FOREVER on purpose: they explain who held which role and why, and point at the anonymised account after deletion.

## Policies

| Policy | Kept | Applied by |
|---|---|---|
| DAYS_90 | 90 days after the event | `HistoryRetentionJob` (nightly) |
| YEARS_2 | 2 years after the event | `HistoryRetentionJob` (nightly) |
| ACCOUNT_LIFETIME | while the account exists; on deletion values, reason and metadata of the entries about the user are cleared | `AccountDeletionService` → `HistoryRetentionService.anonymiseAccountLifetime` |
| FOREVER | not deleted (governance trace); the actor points at the anonymised account after deletion | — |

## Personal data outside the history

| Table | Kept for | Removed by |
|---|---|---|
| `auth_session` | 30 days after expiry or revocation | `AuthRecordsCleanup` |
| `email_change` | 30 days after the link expired | `AuthRecordsCleanup` |
| `ip_block` | 30 days after the block ended (blocks without end stay) | `AuthRecordsCleanup` |
| `registration_attempt` | 90 days | `AuthRecordsCleanup` |
| `login_token` | 1 day after expiry | `LoginTokenCleanup` |
| `oauth_authorization_request` | until expiry plus grace (codes with them) | `OAuthGrantService` |
| `oauth_refresh_token` | until expiry plus grace | `OAuthGrantService` |
| `telegram_link_token` | until expiry | `NotificationCleanup` |
| `push_subscription` | until `app.push.max-failures` deliveries failed | `NotificationCleanup` |
| `bell` | 90 days after read, 30 days after deleted | `NotificationCleanup` |
| `mail_outbox` | 90 days after sending | `NotificationCleanup` |
| `upload` | unclaimed uploads after expiry; claimed ones with their content | `UploadCleanup` |
| `user_archive` | 2 years after the account deletion | `UserArchiveCleanup` |
| `job_run` | 90 days (job results such as data export zips with their expiry) | `CleanupJob` |
| `pickup_policy_audit`, `request_policy_audit`, `open_slot_advert_audit` | `app.automation.audit-retention-days` (7), then anonymous daily counts | `AuditRetentionJob` |
| `signout_cancellation`, `pickup_extra_slot` | `app.automation.signout-retention-days` (180), then anonymous daily counts | `AuditRetentionJob` |
| `basket` | expired baskets stay as statistics (status EXPIRED); contact data and exact home location go on account deletion | `BasketExpiryJob` (status only) |
| `history_entry` | per action, see below | `HistoryRetentionJob` |
| `search_index` | rebuilt nightly | `SearchReindexJob` |

Everything else in `data-inventory.md` is kept while the account exists and handled by the account deletion.
