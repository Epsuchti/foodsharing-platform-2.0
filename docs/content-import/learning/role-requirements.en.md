# Requirements for roles in foodsharing

*As of: 08.11.2026 - 00:00*

There are different roles and tasks at foodsharing. It starts with the foodsharers (that is all of us), and there are further roles that involve a bit more responsibility and for which you therefore have to meet further requirements. They are compiled in this article.

### <a id="allgemeines-fuer-alle-rollen"></a>General points for all roles

1. On request, people at foodsharing receive the following statuses and with them the possibility to take on the corresponding roles, if they meet the requirements given for each.
2. If a requirement no longer applies, the corresponding status is withdrawn; if the requirement is met again, the foodsaver or foodsharer gets the status back.

### <a id="voraussetzungen-fuer-foodsharer-innen"></a>Requirements for foodsharers

- at least 18 years old
- account on the foodsharing platform
- acceptance of the foodsharing basics
- consent to the privacy policy
- no red card

### <a id="voraussetzungen-fuer-unverifizierte-foodsaver-innen"></a>Requirements for unverified foodsavers

- foodsharer
- passed foodsaver quiz
- confirmation of the legal agreement (Germany, Austria) for foodsavers
- no yellow-red card whose duration has not yet expired

### <a id="voraussetzungen-fuer-verifizierte-foodsaver-innen"></a>Requirements for verified foodsavers

- unverified foodsharer
- correct data in the profile: name, address, date of birth, representative photo
- at least 3 successfully completed intro pickups within 6 months
- no yellow card whose duration has not yet expired

### <a id="voraussetzungen-fuer-betriebsverantwortliche"></a>Requirements for store managers

- verified foodsaver
- passed quiz for store managers
- consent to the data protection notice (supplement to the privacy policy)
- consent to the legal agreement for store managers (supplement to the legal agreement)

### <a id="voraussetzungen-fuer-botschafter-innen"></a>Requirements for ambassadors

- verified foodsaver for at least 6 months
- no consequences for relevant rule violations that cannot yet be expunged
- store manager
- passed quiz for ambassadors
- at least 30 pickups at foodsharing
- democratically elected in the district according to the "rules for ambassador elections" at foodsharing
- a welcome talk with the ambassador welcome team
- In the first 2 years after a district is founded, the number of pickups, the time as a verified foodsaver and the election do not apply as requirements for ambassadors.
