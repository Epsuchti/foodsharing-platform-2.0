# Code of conduct

*Valid from 02.01.2020*

**Explanations and reasons**\
For the new code of conduct there is a [page with explanations](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen) that explains for each rule why it is important for foodsharing.

The code of conduct is developed further by the *AG Verhaltensregeln und Meldesystem* (working group code of conduct and reporting system). Suggestions and ideas can be sent to the working group at any time (preferably by email to [verhaltensregeln@foodsharing.network](mailto:verhaltensregeln@foodsharing.network)).\
The working group reviews all ideas and suggestions it receives; the rules in force are planned to change twice a year – usually in May and November.\
Everyone interested is warmly invited to [join the working group](https://foodsharing.de/?page=bezirk&bid=1640).

### <a id="praeambel"></a>Preamble

foodsharing needs a uniform code of conduct for all foodsavers so that, as a large and by now even international movement, it can work together and create and keep a positive image of and trust in us in public – with cooperating businesses, private individuals and in politics.

These rules are therefore the basis for all foodsavers and all foodsharing districts. Given the many different approaches in practice, however, it is not possible to set general rules for every situation for all districts. That is why there are a few rules (B7, C3 in part, C4) that the districts can and should adapt to local conditions and ideas; they are set in italics.

In addition, every district is free to add rules in moderation, as long as the additions do not contradict the other rules.

**The following abbreviations are used in this text**:\
FS = foodsaver, BV = store manager, BOT = ambassador

## <a id="a-allgemeines-verhalten-bei-foodsharing"></a>A) General behaviour at foodsharing

- **A1)** Dealing with other FS is always friendly, respectful and peaceful. [Explanation](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen#Regel_A1.29_.E2.80.93_Freundlicher_Umgang_untereinander)
- **A2)** FS address each other informally ("Du" in German). [Explanation](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen#Regel_A2.29_.E2.80.93_Duzen_als_Standard)
- **A3)** Differences of opinion in a store team should be discussed within the team, within a district within the district. Personal disputes are not carried out in forums, on walls or in the team chat. They are to be settled by private message or in person, or with the support of the groups provided for the district. [Explanation](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen#Regeln_A3.29-A4.29_.E2.80.93_Diskussionen_im_Forum)
- **A4)** In disputes and conflicts, always stay factual and polite, avoid irony and sarcasm, keep a cool head and answer in a considered way, especially in political discussions or on other serious topics. [Explanation](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen#Regeln_A3.29-A4.29_.E2.80.93_Diskussionen_im_Forum)
- **A5)** Private messages and the information in them may only be published or passed on with the permission of the sender. [Explanation](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen#Regel_A5.29_.E2.80.93_Ver.C3.B6ffentlichung_von_privaten_Nachrichten)
- **A6)** Copyrighted works and materials, advertising and spam are not allowed on the online platform.\
  Posts that break one of these rules, and topics unrelated to foodsharing, can be deleted. In working groups their admins decide on deletion, for events the person who created the event, on store walls the store managers. In forums the [Lokale Schiedsstelle](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Lokale_Schiedsstelle) (local arbitration board) decides at the request of a forum member; the deletion is carried out by the ZMG. [Explanation](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen#Regeln_A6.29_und_A7.29_-_Erlaubte_Forumsbeitr.C3.A4ge.2C_L.C3.B6schen_von_Beitr.C3.A4gen)
- **A7)** In forums, before a post is deleted, a private message with the text and the reason for the deletion is sent to the author.\
  Transparency is important. That is why every deleted post is marked as follows: "This post/topic by [...] was deleted because it broke a foodsharing rule of conduct or the topic is not within the scope of foodsharing." [Explanation](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen#Regeln_A6.29_und_A7.29_.E2.80.93_Erlaubte_Forumsbeitr.C3.A4ge.2C_L.C3.B6schen_von_Beitr.C3.A4gen)
- **A8)** FS must enter their name, postal address, date of birth, a profile photo on which they can be recognised and, if available, a phone number (recommended) correctly in their profile on the foodsharing platform, keep them up to date and prove them with an identity document on request. [Explanation](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen#Regel_A8.29_.E2.80.93_Verpflichtung_zur_Angabe_korrekter_pers.C3.B6nlicher_Daten)
- **A9)** Information about internal processes at foodsharing (e.g. personal data of other foodsavers, details of cooperations, pickup times) may only be communicated within foodsharing and only to the foodsavers who need it for their work at foodsharing. [Explanation](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen#Regel_A9.29_-_Vertraulichkeit_von_Foodsaver-Daten_und_foodsharing-internen_Informationen)

## <a id="b-verhalten-bei-abholungen"></a>B) Behaviour during pickups

- **B1)** Punctuality and appropriate behaviour are important at pickups. Bringing animals into the store is forbidden.\
  Bringing children is not allowed. In agreement with the store, BV can allow exceptions for individual FS and individual children.\
  The FS ID card must always be carried during pickups. [Explanation](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen#Regel_B1.29.2C_Satz_1_.E2.80.93_P.C3.BCnktlichkeit_und_Auftreten)
- **B2)** Pickups outside the fixed times are only allowed after agreement with a BV. [Explanation](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen#Regel_B2.29_.E2.80.93_Abholungen_au.C3.9Ferhalb_der_vereinbarten_Termine)
- **B3)** If several FS pick up together, they enter and leave the store together and carry out the pickup without arguing or discussing. [Explanation](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen#Regel_B3.29_.E2.80.93_mehrere_Abholer.2Ainnen)
- **B4)** The FS picking up always take all the food handed over by the store that is still edible. Exceptions can be agreed with the store.\
  All FS take the same amount of food, unless everyone agrees to a different split. [Explanation](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen#Regel_B4.29_.E2.80.93_alle_LM_mitnehmen_.28.3F.29)
- **B5)** During a pickup, only the following may enter the store:\
  – FS with an ID card who are signed up for the slot or step in as a replacement;\
  – prospective FS after accepting the legal agreement, during an intro pickup together with an FS\
  Other people must wait away from the store as "carrying help". [Explanation](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen#Regel_B5.29_.E2.80.93_Wer_darf_abholen_gehen)
- **B6)** During the pickup, be considerate of the store's customers and employees. No discussions are held.\
  The people picking up follow the wishes of the employees and leave the pickup place tidy. [Explanation](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen#Regel_B6.29_.E2.80.93_Verhalten_im_Betrieb)
- **B7)** *A waiting time requested by the store (deviation from the agreed time) of up to 15 minutes must be accepted; after that, the pickup can be politely cancelled.* [Explanation](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen#Regel_B7.29_.E2.80.93_Wartezeit)
- **B8)** If the employees of a store behave unfriendly, the people picking up stay polite anyway; afterwards they can inform one of the BV. [Explanation](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen#Regel_B8.29_.E2.80.93_Keine_Diskussionen_mit_Mitarbeitenden)
- **B9)** Comments on the amount, type and use of the food saved by the FS are to be avoided. [Explanation](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen#Regel_B9.29_-_Wenn_nichts_zu_retten_ist)
- **B10)** No food may be sold or used for barter, not even food made from saved food. A deposit for crates, bottles or the like may be accepted or asked for if it was paid at the pickup. [Explanation](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen#Regel_B10.29_-_kein_Verkauf.2C_kein_Tauschen)
- **B11)** Pickups should use means that are as ecologically gentle as possible. Long car journeys and the unnecessary use of disposable items should be avoided where possible. If the pickup is possible without a car, the BV should provide enough slots to make this kind of pickup possible and encourage it. [Explanation](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen#Regel_B11.29_.E2.80.93_.C3.B6kologische_Mittel)
- **B12)** The goal at foodsharing is to save food from the bin. The people picking up decide independently to whom the food is passed on. Need is not decisive. [Explanation](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen#Regel_B12.29_.E2.80.93_nicht_nur_an_Bed.C3.BCrftige)
- **B13)** Everyone picking up should try to use up or share ("fairteilen") all the saved food (with people, if necessary also with animals). [Explanation](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen#Regel_B13.29_.E2.80.93_gerettete_Lebensmittel_wegwerfen.3F)

## <a id="c-betriebe-betriebsteam-planung-von-abholungen"></a>C) Stores, store team, planning pickups

- **C1)** Before a pickup, FS must know the current version of the store's particulars as well as current wall posts and messages in the team chat. [Explanation](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen#Regel_C1.29_.E2.80.93_aktuelle_Informationen_lesen_vor_Abholungen)
- **C2)** All pickups at stores are coordinated via the store's page on the foodsharing online platform. For individual stores, other communication channels can be used in addition in agreement with the BOTs, as long as no team member is excluded by this. [Explanation](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen#Regel_C2.29_.E2.80.93_Organisation_nur_.C3.BCber_die_foodsharing-Plattform)
- **C3)** Whoever signs up for a pickup slot is responsible for the pickup. Whoever cannot make a pickup slot in exceptional cases must sign out of the slot immediately *and look for a replacement by message to the team.\
  If cancelling less than 24 hours before the slot, the FS must look for a replacement by message to the team and by phone and, in an emergency, inform a BV by phone.* [Explanation](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen#Regel_C3.29_.E2.80.93_Austragen_aus_Abholterminen)
- **C4)** *Messages from a store's employees to the people picking up must be passed on directly to at least one BV (recommendation).* [Explanation](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen#Regel_C4_.E2.80.93_Mitteilungen_des_Betriebs)
- **C5)** Special arrangements for a store are always agreed with the store by the BV and set down in the particulars on the store page. [Explanation](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen#Regel_C5.29_.E2.80.93_Festlegungen_f.C3.BCr_Abholungen_in_einem_Betrieb)
- **C6)** New stores may only be approached on one's own initiative by people who have passed the BV quiz, in agreement with a BOT and, for store chains, with the responsible KAM or the store chains group. [Explanation](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen#Regel_C6.29_.E2.80.93_Ansprechen_von_Betrieben)
- **C7)** Before approaching a store, it must be checked whether the store has already been entered on the online platform; if not, it must be entered. [Explanation](https://wiki.foodsharing.network/wiki/Verhaltensregeln_-_Erl%C3%A4uterungen#Regel_C7.29_.E2.80.93_neue_Betriebe_eintragen_auf_Plattform)
