# EA23 "Einführungen": one page per region for intro pickups (EinAbs)

Status: the date check is approved (mockup and all of the designer's recommendations, owner 2026-11-10). **Still open: Q1, Q2, Q4, Q5** (end of this file); the plan follows the recommendations until the owner answers. Owner todo: "Refactor the intro pickups" (`todo-eric.md`). Date check design: `docs/design/einab-date-check-proposal.md` and `-mockup.html`, the source for its states, tokens, i18n texts and test ids. A mockup of the whole page is still advised.

## Goal
Guides and user managers handle EinAbs on one page per region, `/regions/:regionId/intro-pickups/<tab>`, with four tabs: **Termine** (dates with their bookings inline), **Neue** (funnel plus a per-newcomer date check), **Begleiter:innen** (guides, with your own settings in your own row) and **Regeln** (the region's EinAb rules, with import and "Nachträge" under "Mehr"). The role decides what each tab shows. The URL does not.

## Decisions
- D1 (owner): the four tabs above. Old routes redirect: `/guide` → `dates`, `/guide/settings` → `guides`, `/regions/:id/newcomers` → `newcomers`, `/regions/:id/intro-pickups/admin[?tab=guides|bookings]` → `guides` or `dates`. `/regions/:id/intro-pickups/import` stays as a sub-page (back link → `rules`).
- D2 (owner): the per-newcomer date check replaces the 16-row gap table. Removed: `NewcomerFunnel.gaps`, `FunnelGap`, `GapSeverity`, and the four totals `openSlots`, `introPickupsStillNeeded`, `openFairteilerSlots`, `fairteilerVisitsStillNeeded`. The totals move into the date check (D3c).
- D3 (owner, the designer's recommendations 1–8):
  - (a) Stuck newcomers are grouped by need. A need is a set of conditions out of `OTHER_GUIDE`, `FAIRTEILER`, `CATEGORY` and `ANY`. One date that must meet two rules is one card with a combined title. A person is in two cards only when they need two separate dates; the card shows an "auch bei" link to the other one.
  - (b) The action is "Termin anbieten" (same wording as `introPickups.guide.create`). It opens `einab-form-dialog` prefilled: places = min(people, 3), category = the first missing one, Fairteiler checked when needed. A purpose banner warns when an edit breaks the fit; saving is never blocked.
  - (c) The two totals ("places vs. needed", "Fairteiler places vs. needed") move into the verdict card and are removed from the top stat row.
  - (d) No button for a guide the group already had (note "a date by you would not count") or for a viewer without the guide right in the region (note plus a link to the guides tab). No "create on behalf of a guide".
  - (e) Guides see counts only; names only with `CAN_MANAGE_USERS` (owner, this was Q3).
  - (f) An `OTHER_GUIDE` card lists "Anbieten könnten: …": the active guides of the region whom nobody in the group has had. The server sends this list.
  - (g) The "Registriert in" filter does not affect the check: it always covers everyone in training.
  - (h) No notification to the group members when the date is saved (out of scope).
- D4 Regions: the page's region is the route param. The nav entry "Einführungen" points to `/guide`, which resolves the region in this order: the active region (if you may use the page there), then the home region, then the first region from the session permissions (`CAN_GIVE_INTRO_PICKUPS@r` or `CAN_MANAGE_USERS@r`). With no such region you see the existing "no regions" empty state. If you guide in more than one region, the page header has a region select. Your own settings are global, and their row says "gilt für alle deine Bezirke".
- D5 Permissions stay as they are (Q1 may change that):
  - Plain guide: own dates in Termine with all guide actions, funnel and date check as counts, Begleiter:innen = `listAssignableGuides` plus your own settings row, no Regeln tab.
  - `CAN_MANAGE_USERS` (FSMANAGEMENT function, direct grant, ORGA): all dates (`listRegionIntroPickups`), delete with reason, counted, admin comments, names, the full guide administration, Regeln read-only, "Mehr" (import, Nachträge).
  - Region administrators (`mayAdministrateRegion`): can also release guides, grant guide admin rights and save the rules.
  - Actions on a date (edit, assign, book for, cancel, done, count) stay limited to the date's own guide, or ORGA.
- D6 Termine: "Nur meine" is on at the start for guides and off for managers who are not guides. For plain guides, switching it off shows the other guides' dates from `listPublicIntroPickups` (date, city, free places; Q2). The "counted" toggle moves into the slot row of past dates.
- D7 Contract changes (no database change; no permission change unless Q1 = yes):
  - (a) new `GET /api/regions/{regionId}/date-check` (`getDateCheck`, tag `intro-pickup-guide`, no `days` parameter, per D3g);
  - (b) `NewcomerFunnel` loses the fields named in D2;
  - (c) optional `counted` on `IntroPickupSlot`, set by the same mapper rule as `GuideBooking.counted`.
- D8 Server links in bells and mails (`/guide/intro-pickups[/<id>]`, which no client route matches today, and `/intro-pickups/admin[?tab=guides]`) change to the new URLs. Client redirects also catch old links in bells that are already stored.

## Scope
In: the page and its tabs, the redirects, the nav entry, the date check (server and client), removing the gap table, moving the EinAb card from the region's Einstellungen tab to Regeln, bell and mail links, i18n in five languages, tests, screenshots, docs.
Out: the other planner's fix batch (FAIRTEILER_MISSING stage label, booking list showing only bookable dates, notifications only for bookable dates, nav cleanup for new users, one date format), which lands first. Also out: the newcomer pages (`/intro-pickups`, `/me/intro-pickups`, public slots), the guide quiz rules, allocation between newcomers competing for places, notifying the group (D3h), creating dates on behalf of a guide.

## Changes
**Contract** (`server/src/main/resources/openapi/`): `paths/intro-pickup-guide.yaml` gets `getDateCheck` (403/404). `schemas/intro-pickups.yaml`:
- `DateCheck {status: OK|PARTIAL|NONE, horizonDays, inTraining, canFinish, stuck, noDates, fairteilerRequired, openSlots, placesStillNeeded, openFairteilerSlots, fairteilerVisitsStillNeeded, groups: NeedGroup[]}`
- `NeedGroup {id, kinds: NeedKind[], categories: IntroPickupCategory[] (the missing ones, CATEGORY only), visitFairteiler, excludedGuides: UserSummary[], possibleGuides: UserSummary[], blockedDates, openDates, peopleCount, neededTotal, bookableTotal, people: NeedPerson[] (empty without names), placesRequired, datesRequired, viewerExcluded, viewerCanOffer}`
- `NeedPerson {user, needed, bookable, alsoIn: string[]}` (the other group ids)
- Remove what D2 lists, add `IntroPickupSlot.counted`, then regenerate the server and `client/src/app/api`.

**Server** (`network.foodsharing.intropickup`):
- `service/DateCheck.java` (new, pure static, like `NewcomerProgressService.funnel`):
  1. Can finish: for each newcomer in training with `remainingToBook() > 0`, a depth-first search over the open EinAbs in the horizon (AVAILABLE slots) applying `BookingRules.check(Booker(true,true,false), true, pickup, bookings + hypothetical BOOKED, rules)` up to `remainingToBook`. `bookable` = the depth reached.
  2. Needs: for each stuck person, a candidate date shape = category (not ignored) × Fairteiler (never with ONLINE) × "guide not among the person's earlier guides" (only with `preventDuplicateGuideBookings`). A shape helps if adding it raises the depth. A person who is short by k dates gets up to k shapes, and so up to k cards.
  3. Grouping: a greedy cover picks the shape that helps the most people first (ties: fewer conditions, then category order). The group's `kinds` are the conditions that shape needs beyond "any free place".
  4. Per group: `excludedGuides` (union), `possibleGuides` (active guides of the region minus excluded), `blockedDates`/`openDates` (for the `why.OTHER_GUIDE` text), `placesRequired` = people, `datesRequired` = ceil(people / 3), `viewerExcluded`, `viewerCanOffer` (the viewer is an active guide here and not excluded).
- `service/DateCheckService.java` (new, `@Transactional(readOnly = true)`):
  - Access: guide or `CAN_MANAGE_USERS` in the region, as the funnel; names only with `CAN_MANAGE_USERS`.
  - Newcomer selection: the same as the funnel, but ignoring `days`. It goes through a package-private loader shared with `NewcomerProgressService.funnel`.
  - Bookings: built like `IntroPickupBookingService.bookingsOf`, shared, not copied.
  - Horizon: `app.intropickup.gap-horizon-days`.
- `NewcomerProgressService`: delete `gaps()`, `severity()`, `Gap`, `GapSeverity` and the moved totals. `web/IntroPickupGuideController` implements `getDateCheck`. `mapper/IntroPickupMapper` maps `DateCheck` and `IntroPickupSlot.counted`.
- Links: `IntroPickupNotifications` (~lines 92, 103, 132, 182) → `/regions/<rid>/intro-pickups/dates?date=<id>`; `GuideQuizNotifications:56` → `…/guides`; `GuideQuizTransitionService:147` → `…/dates`.

## Risks
- Collisions with the fix batch: `NewcomerProgressService.java` (stage label), `newcomer-funnel-page.component.ts` (moves into `newcomers.tab.ts`), `IntroPickupBookingService.java` (shared `bookingsOf`), `IntroPickupNotifications.java`, `layout/nav.config.ts` + `sidenav.component.spec.ts`, `einab-slots`/guide/admin pages (date format), `intro-pickups/i18n/*.json`. Rebase on the batch first.
- The check looks at each newcomer alone, so two people may count on the same last free place. `placesRequired` counts people.
- Search cost: newcomers × open dates^remaining (≤ the booking limit, usually 3). Prune identical shapes; a unit test with 200 newcomers and 40 dates must stay under 200 ms.
- The "Buchungen" list by person goes away (Q5).
- Own settings are global but shown inside a region's page. The row says so.
